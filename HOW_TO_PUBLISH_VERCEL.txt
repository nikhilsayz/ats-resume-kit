PUT THIS PAGE ONLINE WITH VERCEL (free, about 15 minutes)

STEP 1. EDIT 3 LINES
Open index.html in Notepad (right-click > Open with > Notepad).
Near the top, change only the text inside the quotes:
  BUY_URL       = your Topmate link for the kit (the 144 product)
  PIXEL_ID      = your Meta Pixel ID, numbers only
  SUPPORT_EMAIL = your email
Press Ctrl + S.

STEP 2. PUT THE FILES ON GITHUB
1. Go to github.com and log in (or sign up free).
2. Click + (top right) > New repository.
3. Name: ats-resume-kit. Keep it Public. Click Create repository.
4. On the next page click "uploading an existing file".
5. Open the unzipped "site" folder on your laptop. Select EVERYTHING inside it
   (index.html, the img folder, the fonts folder, vercel.json) and drag it into the browser.
   Do not drag the "site" folder itself, only what is inside it.
6. Click Commit changes.

STEP 3. CONNECT VERCEL
1. Go to vercel.com and log in with GitHub.
2. Click Add New > Project.
3. Find ats-resume-kit and click Import.
4. Framework Preset: Other. Do not change anything else. Click Deploy.
5. After about 30 seconds you get a link like ats-resume-kit.vercel.app.

STEP 4. TEST ON YOUR PHONE
1. Open the link. Check that the fonts look bold and condensed and the images load.
2. Tap "Get the kit". It must open your Topmate product.
3. In Meta Events Manager > your Pixel > Test events, open the page and tap Buy.
   You should see PageView and InitiateCheckout.

TO CHANGE SOMETHING LATER
Edit index.html on GitHub (click the file > pencil icon > Commit changes).
Vercel updates the live page by itself within a minute.
